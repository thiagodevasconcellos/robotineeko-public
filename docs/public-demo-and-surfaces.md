# Public Demo And Surfaces

Robotineeko has more than one product surface, but not every surface is meant
for the same audience or the same level of access.

This document explains the public-facing shape of the product without exposing
private operational details.

## Main Public Surface

The main public-facing surface is the web console.

That surface is where a reviewer can understand the product structure, inspect
curated examples, and see how the workflow is organized.

It is the broadest public entry point into the product.

## Demo Philosophy

The demo is designed to be honest and safe.

That means it favors:

- real product structure
- guided inspection
- curated examples
- limited mutation power

It does not try to simulate full unrestricted operator access.

## What A Reviewer Can Understand

A reviewer should be able to understand:

- how the main console is organized
- which surfaces are used for strategy work
- how simulation and review fit together
- how documentation supports the product workflow

The point of the public demo is product comprehension, not operational control.

## Mobile Companion

Robotineeko also includes a lighter companion surface for follow-up and
monitoring-oriented use cases.

It should be understood as a companion to the main console, not as a second
full editing environment.

## Why Access Is Intentionally Limited

Some capabilities are intentionally restricted outside private operator use.

That is by design.

The public-facing experience should help an employer or reviewer inspect the
product safely without exposing:

- restricted operational workflows
- durable private state
- sensitive product areas
- real internal control surfaces

## Public Versus Private Boundaries

Public-facing material may include:

- curated examples
- workflow explanations
- product-level documentation
- screenshots and diagrams

Private material remains outside the public repository and outside the public
demo when it belongs to:

- internal operations
- restricted data areas
- sensitive runtime behavior
- production-only maintenance concerns

## Summary

The public demo exists to show how the product thinks.

It is intentionally strong on:

- structure
- clarity
- workflow
- product intent

It is intentionally narrow on:

- private control
- sensitive operations
- internal-only surfaces
