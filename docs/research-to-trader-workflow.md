# Research To Trader Workflow

Robotineeko is designed as a workflow, not a pile of unrelated tabs.

## Intended progression

1. `Strategy` defines the idea.
2. `Backtester` measures it under explicit assumptions.
3. `Results` explains what happened.
4. `Research` compares alternatives and keeps continuity.
5. `Trader` receives only the ideas that are mature enough for runtime use.

## Why the separation matters

The product intentionally avoids collapsing everything into one screen where interesting-looking results jump straight into runtime. Each surface has a stricter role in the promotion funnel.

## Research philosophy

Positive checkpoints are useful evidence, but not proof by themselves. The system is built to preserve promising work, compare it honestly, and only then let it influence runtime operation.
